2019-03-25

From now on, I'll try to avoid writing superficial articles and basic tutorials. While such articles have been very helpful during my learning process, there are already many more detailed and comprehensive articles online. There's really no need to spend time on these anymore. Going forward, I'll delve into source code and explore the implementation principles behind common usage, hoping to document my journey of improving technical abilities.

2018-02-27

During my learning journey, the internet has provided me with tremendous support and help. However, it's undeniable that there are many "mutually referenced" materials on the Chinese internet, which inevitably include a lot of unverified information that can be quite harmful.

Today, I wasted an entire evening due to such issues. And this is just what I've discovered with obvious errors - who knows how many others remain undetected.

Unfortunately, before this, I also wrote many articles that lacked deep thinking and were simply "borrowed." It's quite embarrassing. From now on, I will only write technical articles after I truly understand them, and every point must have corresponding references. I'd rather write less than write carelessly. Previous articles will also need to be gradually corrected.
